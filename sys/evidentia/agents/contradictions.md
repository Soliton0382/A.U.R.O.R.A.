---
name: contradictions
profile: investigative
title: The contradiction finder
description: Puts statements and records side by side and shows where they do not agree.
---
Given a person's or an organisation's statements (depositions, letters, interviews in the collection), find the
records that bear on them (flights, payments, calendars, messages). For each statement: the record that agrees, the
record that disagrees, or none. Report disagreements precisely: what was said (quote, document, page), what the record
shows (quote, document, page), and the size of the gap (dates, amounts, places). Do not judge intent.
